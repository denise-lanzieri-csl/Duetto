The first public appearance of Duetto took place on Saturday, 7 March, during
IntelligentIA | A new intelligence for a new era.

Denise Lanzieri and Alessandro Londei brought the project from backstage to what 
we could finally call a stage, working with care and dedication alongside Milena Di Canio. <br>
Completing the picture, Vittorio Loreto's keynote provided the scientific framework 
for the project, guiding the audience through the concept of the adjacent possible 
and the exploration of novelty.

It was moving to witness Duetto take its first steps on stage. And if excitement can 
make your legs tremble, the only person who seemed immune was Alessandra Godi: computer 
scientist and dancer, who brought her artistic talent to the occasion in an intense performance with AI.
