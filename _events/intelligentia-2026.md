The first public appearance of Duetto took place on Saturday, 7 March, during
IntelligentIA | A new intelligence for a new era.

Denise Lanzieri and Alessandro Londei guided the project from backstage to what
we can truly call a stage, with great care and precision, together with Milena
Di Canio.

Closing the circle, the keynote speech by Vittorio Loreto provided the project's
scientific framework, leading the audience through the theory of the adjacent
possible and the exploration of novelty.

It was moving to witness Duetto's first steps on its first stage. And if
excitement can make legs tremble, the only one who didn't seem affected was
Alessandra Godi: computer scientist and dancer, who for this occasion chose her
artistic skills to give us an intense moment of dancing with AI.
