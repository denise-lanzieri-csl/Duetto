The map

Every cell represents a learned prototype in Duetto's posture vocabulary: a body configuration the model can use to represent movement. Nearby cells represent similar postures, revealing the structure of this learned space. Hover over a cell to see the corresponding body configuration from the front and side.

The dial

One dial, four settings. Adjust it to explore how closely Duetto follows the performer’s movement, from more faithful responses to greater creative freedom.
