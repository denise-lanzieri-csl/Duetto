The map

Every cell below is one entry in the tokenizer's vocabulary: a posture the model can use. Neighbouring cells hold similar postures, which is what makes the space navigable rather than arbitrary. Hover a cell to see the body it stands for, front and side.

The dial

One dial, six settings. Move it and watch how far the system is allowed to wander from the body it is listening to.
